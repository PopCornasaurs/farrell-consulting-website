Services Built for Small Businesses

📒

Monthly Bookkeeping

Best for: Established businesses that want accurate, up-to-date books every month without the stress.

  • Monthly account reconciliation
  • Transaction categorization
  • Monthly financial reports (P&L, Balance Sheet)
  • Ongoing communication & support
🔍

Catch-Up & Cleanup Bookkeeping

Best for: Businesses that are behind on their books — whether it's a few months or a few years.

  • Backlog reconciliation (any timeframe)
  • Error identification & correction
  • Chart of accounts clean-up
  • Handoff to monthly bookkeeping when ready
🎓

Training & Consultation

Best for: Business owners who manage their own books and want to learn QuickBooks, set up their system correctly, or get expert guidance when questions arise.

  • QuickBooks setup, review & optimization
  • 1-on-1 training tailored to your business
  • Answers to your bookkeeping & accounting questions
  • Ongoing support as your business grows
📊

Accounts Receivable & Accounts Payable Tracking

Best for: Businesses that need a clear picture of what they're owed and what they owe — so cash flow never catches you off guard.

  • Invoice tracking & collections follow-up
  • Bill management & vendor payment tracking
  • Aging reports (AR & AP)
  • Cash flow visibility & reporting
💼

Payroll Processing

Best for: Business owners who want payroll handled accurately and on time — without the headache of doing it themselves.

  • Full-service payroll runs
  • Payroll tax calculations & filings
  • Direct deposit & pay stub management
  • QuickBooks Payroll integration & support
