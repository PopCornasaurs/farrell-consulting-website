About Farrell Consulting

We believe every small business owner deserves financial clarity — without the complexity or the big-firm price tag.

Meet the Owner

Hi, I'm Christian Farrell — the founder and bookkeeper behind Farrell Consulting LLC.

My journey into bookkeeping started over six years ago, and it didn't take long for me to fall in love with the craft. I built my foundation as a full-charge bookkeeper before stepping into a Controller role at a commercial insurance agency, where I gained a deep understanding of what it really takes to keep a business financially healthy. That experience gave me something invaluable — the ability to see the numbers not just as figures, but as the story of your business.

I started Farrell Consulting LLC because I kept seeing the same problem: business owners drowning in disorganized books, unsure if their numbers were right, and spending time on finances instead of doing what they actually love. That's where I come in. My mission is simple — give you clean, accurate books in a timely manner, take the stress off your plate, and give you back the time and confidence to focus on growing your business.

When I'm not crunching numbers, you'll find me on the soccer field or the golf course, diving into a philosophy book, or spending time with my dog, Stella.

Your business deserves books you can count on — and an owner who genuinely cares about your success. Let's work together.
